In 2009 four independent states (Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Türkiye) signed the Nakhchivan Agreement and formed the Turkic Council. Uzbekistan joined in 2019, and in 2021 it was renamed the Organization of Turkic States. It is cooperation between sovereign countries, not one state: the octopus merge in the graph.

## Commits on this branch

Each commit is one historical event.

- **2009**: Nakhchivan Agreement: the Turkic Council
- **2009**: Octopus merge: Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Turkey found the Turkic Council (cooperation, not one state)
- **2019**: Uzbekistan joins the Turkic Council
- **2021**: Renamed the Organization of Turkic States (Istanbul summit)

Run `git log --oneline turkic-states` to see them together with the history they build on. The same list is in [`lineages/turkic-states.md`](lineages/turkic-states.md).
