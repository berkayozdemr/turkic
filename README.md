# Seljuks

`oghuz/seljuk`

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where they ruled** | From Central Asia across Iran and Iraq to Anatolia |
| **Branch** | Oghuz: Southwestern Turkic, carried west by the Seljuks from the 11th century. |

A dynasty from the Qiniq tribe of the Oghuz. Tughril and Chaghri Beg founded the Great Seljuk state in 1037 and defeated the Ghaznavids at Dandanaqan in 1040. The victory at Manzikert in 1071 opened Anatolia to Turkic settlement, and the Sultanate of Rum ruled there from 1077 to the early 14th century. The Oghuz who came west with the Seljuks are the linguistic ancestors of today's Turks and Azerbaijanis.
