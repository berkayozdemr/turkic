# Turks

`oghuz/turkish`

| | |
|---|---|
| **Self-name** | Türk |
| **Where they live today** | Türkiye and Northern Cyprus; the Balkans; diaspora in western Europe |
| **Branch** | Oghuz: Southwestern Turkic, carried west by the Seljuks from the 11th century. |

The largest Turkic people, with about 85 million speakers. Heirs of the Oghuz who entered Anatolia after Manzikert (1071), of the Seljuks of Rum and of the Ottoman state. The Republic of Türkiye was founded in 1923 and switched to a Latin alphabet in 1928.

## Commits on this branch

Each commit is one historical event.

- **1077**: Seljuk Sultanate of Rum in Anatolia
- **1299**: Osman I founds the Ottoman beylik (traditional date)
- **1453**: Ottoman conquest of Constantinople
- **1923**: Republic of Turkey proclaimed (29 October)
- **1928**: Latin-based Turkish alphabet adopted

Run `git log --oneline oghuz/turkish` to see them together with the history they build on. The same list is in [`lineages/oghuz--turkish.md`](lineages/oghuz--turkish.md).
