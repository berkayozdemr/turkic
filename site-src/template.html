The Turkic family, as a git graph

$ git log --graph --all # c. 2,000 years · 6 branches + the Old Uyghur line

One proto-language on the eastern steppe: forked, archived and merged across Eurasia. Each branch is a living (or archived) Turkic people.

Click any branch, name or event to open its details

turkic-states · 2009 octopus merge: Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Türkiye form the Turkic Council (+ Uzbekistan 2019), since 2021 the Organization of Turkic States. Cooperation between sovereign states, not one state.

A teaching metaphor. Branches follow the linguistic classification of the languages (after Johanson 1998), not ancestry or politics. Fork points are approximate, mostly first attestations, and the order in which the Common Turkic branches split is not settled. “Siberian” is a regional grouping, not a proven single branch. Peoples mixed far more than a graph shows.
